SoundCatch

Record, cut, export.

SoundCatch is an audio recorder: it records the sound that comes out of an app you pick (or of the whole Mac), or opens any audio or video file. You see the waveform, select what you do not need with the mouse, listen to it, delete it, and export one clean file — for instance the soundtrack of a photo slideshow. The app is in French.

What SoundCatch does

  • Records the sound of an app picked from a list (or of the whole Mac), with no microphone and no audio driver to install.
  • Opens a file, audio or video: drop it on the window.
  • Cuts: select with the mouse, listen, delete; several selections at once; a 20 ms crossfade at each joint so there is no click; undo and redo.
  • Spots suspect passages (silences, changes of loudness or timbre, speech) and suggests them: nothing is cut without you.
  • Exports to AAC (.m4a), MP3, Apple Lossless or FLAC, in the folder you choose. The sound stays at studio quality until the export.
