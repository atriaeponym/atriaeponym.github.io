PRICES · INDICES · TOP MOVERS

Stock Cockpit is a free stock market app for Android and Windows PCs. It shows the prices of shares listed on Euronext Paris, including the CAC 40, and of major European, US and UK stocks, in euros and delayed by 15 minutes. At the top of the screen, the day's trend of the main indices; in the top movers, the 5 biggest gainers and the 5 biggest losers of each index. You sort your shares into lists and share them with a simple link. No ads, no account, no tracking.

Share prices from the Paris Stock Exchange and the major markets

Search for a share by its name or its ticker and add it to your list: its last price, its change on the day and the session's curve appear on the home screen.

  • Euronext: Paris, including Euronext Growth and Euronext Access, as well as Amsterdam, Brussels, Lisbon and Milan, with the delayed prices published by Euronext.
  • US and UK shares, and shares from other European countries: quoted in euros on Tradegate, in Berlin.

Every price shows its source, the time it was taken and the label “différé 15 min” (delayed 15 min).

A clear chart for every share

Tap a share: its price, its change in euros and in percent, the open, the day's high and low and the previous close. The chart has a price scale, a time axis, and its high and low marked.

It covers the current session and the week, then one month, six months, one year, five years and ten years, as the share's history builds up.

The day's trend of the CAC 40 and the main indices

At the top of the screen: CAC 40, Dow Jones, S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, DAX, Euro Stoxx 50 and FTSE 100, with their change on the day and the state of their exchange (open, or when it opens).

An index cannot be traded: its trend is measured on an index fund (ETF) that tracks it, quoted in euros on Tradegate. It may therefore differ slightly from the index itself, for example with the euro exchange rate for US and UK indices.

Top movers of the CAC 40 and other indices

The “Palmarès” tab (top movers) ranks the stocks of an index over the last session: the 5 biggest gainers and the 5 biggest losers of the CAC 40, the Dow Jones, the Nasdaq 100, the DAX, the Euro Stoxx 50 and the FTSE 100. The app remembers the index you chose.

Your share lists, shared with a link

  • Several lists with names of your choice (“My list”, “Dividends”…), to create and rename.
  • Sharing with a simple link: the person who receives it opens Stock Cockpit and adds the list in one tap. The link contains only the list's name and the shares' tickers.
  • On Android, a copy of your lists can be kept in your phone's Google backup, so that you get them back after reinstalling. Atria.Eponym has no access to it.
  • On a Windows PC, sharing copies the link to the clipboard, ready to paste into an email or a message; to add a list you received, paste its link into “Importer une liste” (Import a list).

What Stock Cockpit does not do

  • No advice: the app informs, it recommends neither buying nor selling. Always check a price with your broker before placing an order.
  • No instant quotes: prices are delayed by at least 15 minutes and updated every minute.
  • No trading: Stock Cockpit is not a broker.
  • No portfolio tracking: lists hold neither quantities nor purchase prices.

Your data

  • No account, no ads, no analytics tools.
  • Your lists stay on your phone or your PC.
  • When you search for a share, only the text you type goes to the app's service, with nothing that identifies you.

The details are in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stock Cockpit free?

Yes. Stock Cockpit is free, with no ads and no account. On Android, a donation is possible from the “À propos” (About) screen; it does not unlock any feature.

Are the prices delayed?

Yes. Prices are delayed by at least 15 minutes and updated every minute. The time of the price and its source are always shown next to it.

Which stock exchanges are covered?

Euronext Paris, including Euronext Growth and Euronext Access, Euronext Amsterdam, Brussels, Lisbon and Milan, as well as US and UK shares and shares from other European countries quoted in euros on Tradegate.

Can I see the CAC 40 top movers?

Yes. The “Palmarès” tab shows the 5 biggest gainers and the 5 biggest losers of the last session for the CAC 40, the Dow Jones, the Nasdaq 100, the DAX, the Euro Stoxx 50 and the FTSE 100.

Does Stock Cockpit give investment advice?

No. The data is provided for information only and does not constitute investment advice. Always check a price with your broker before placing an order.

How do I share a list of shares?

Open the list's menu and share it: the app creates a link containing the list's name and the shares' tickers, with no prices and nothing about you. The person who opens it adds the list in one tap. On a Windows PC, the link is copied to the clipboard; to add a list you received, paste its link into “Importer une liste” (Import a list).

Which devices does Stock Cockpit work on?

On Android 8 or later, through Google Play, and on PCs with Windows 10 (version 1809) or Windows 11, through the Microsoft Store. The app's interface is in French.
