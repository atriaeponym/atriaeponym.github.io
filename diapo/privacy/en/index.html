Privacy policy — Diapo

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Français

Diapo shows the photos of a folder or a whole disk full screen, subfolders included, on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no data sent over the Internet. Your photos stay where they are: Diapo reads them and never edits, moves or deletes them.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Atria.Eponym, an individual developer based in France. Contact: atria.eponym@gmail.com

Your photos

Diapo reads the photos of the folders, disks or photo library you open, on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Thumbnails are kept in memory while they are shown and never saved.

What Diapo saves on your device

  • Its settings: the display mode, the thumbnail size, and the last photo seen in each folder to come back to it.
  • Your favorites: the list of photos you marked with a heart (where they are, not the photos themselves).
  • Your selections: their name, their destination folder and the list of photos marked and waiting to be copied.
  • Access permissions to the folders and disks you chose, to reopen them without asking again.
  • The free trial: its start date, to count the days left.

All of this stays in the app's own storage on your device and goes away with it.

Selection copies and sharing

When you ask for it, Diapo copies the photos of a selection into the folder you chose. The originals are not touched, no existing file is replaced, and nothing is deleted. When you share a photo, the system (share sheet, clipboard) passes it to the app you pick; Diapo itself sends nothing.

Online drives

If you open a Google Drive, OneDrive or iCloud Drive folder, the service itself, installed on your device, downloads the photos to show, under your account with it. Diapo receives and sends nothing to these services.

Free trial and purchase

Diapo is free during the trial period, then bought once. Payment is handled entirely by your device's store (App Store, Microsoft Store or Google Play), under your account with it: Atria.Eponym receives neither your card, nor your name, nor your e-mail address. The app only asks the store whether Diapo was bought.

Permissions

  • Access to the photos, folders and disks you open: to show their photos, and to copy a selection into the folder you chose.

Third-party services

  • Apple, Microsoft and Google: distribution of the app and payment of the purchase, depending on your device.

Your rights

Diapo collects no personal data: there is nothing to look up or delete at Atria.Eponym. Deleting the app deletes its settings, your favorites and your selections; the copy folders stay yours. For any question: atria.eponym@gmail.com. You may also contact the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
