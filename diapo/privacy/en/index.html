Privacy policy — Diapo

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Français

Diapo is a Mac app that shows the photos of a folder or a whole disk full screen, subfolders included.

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no data sent over the Internet. Your photos stay where they are: Diapo reads them and never edits, moves or deletes them.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Atria.Eponym, an individual developer based in France. Contact: atria.eponym@gmail.com

Your photos

Diapo reads the photos of the folders and disks you open, on your Mac. They are never sent anywhere. Thumbnails are kept in memory while they are shown and never saved to disk.

What Diapo saves on your Mac

  • Its settings: the display mode, the thumbnail size, and the last photo seen in each folder to come back to it.
  • Your favorites: the list of photos you marked with a heart (where they are, not the photos themselves).
  • Your selections: their name, their destination folder and the list of photos marked and waiting to be copied.

All of this stays in the app's folder on your Mac (Library › Application Support) and goes away with it.

Selection copies

When you ask for it, Diapo copies the photos of a selection into the folder you chose. The originals are not touched, no existing file is replaced, and nothing is deleted. What you then do with that folder (sharing it, sending it) does not go through Diapo.

Online drives

If you open a Google Drive, OneDrive or iCloud Drive folder, the service itself, installed on your Mac, downloads the photos to show, under your account with it. Diapo receives and sends nothing to these services.

Donations

You may, if you wish, make a donation from the About screen. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store, under your Apple account: Atria.Eponym receives neither your card, nor your name, nor your e-mail address, and the app sends no information about your donation. A donation unlocks no feature.

Permissions

  • Access to the folders and disks you open: to show their photos, and to copy a selection into the folder you chose.

Third-party services

  • Apple: distribution of the app and, if you make one, payment of donations.

Your rights

Diapo collects no personal data: there is nothing to look up or delete at Atria.Eponym. Deleting the app deletes its settings, your favorites and your selections; the copy folders stay yours. For any question: atria.eponym@gmail.com. You may also contact the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
