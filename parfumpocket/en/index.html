Perfume Price Trackerperfume price comparison

DELIVERED PRICE · HISTORY · ALERTS

Finding cheap perfume starts with knowing where it costs least, delivery included. Perfume Price Tracker is a perfume price comparison app for Android and iPhone: it compares the price of your eau de parfum or eau de toilette across several online shops, follows how that price moves day after day and sends you an alert when it drops. The deal comes to you, with no comparing by hand.

Free · no ads · no account

Get it onGoogle PlayAndroid 8 or later Download on theApp StoreiPhone, iOS 17 or later

Compare a perfume's price across shops, delivery included

The price on display is not the price you pay. Perfume Price Tracker ranks every offer by total delivered price: the price of the bottle plus the cheapest home delivery, for one item. When a shop does not publish its delivery costs, the app says so instead of guessing.

  • The exact size: 30, 50, 100 ml… each size is compared with itself, never a 50 ml against a 100 ml.
  • The price per ml, to see which size works out cheaper.
  • Availability in each shop: in stock, out of stock or unknown.
  • The date of the reading under every price; a price that is too old is greyed out and never shown as current.
  • Public prices only: no promo codes, no members-only prices.

Shops followed today: MyOrigines, Parfumdo and Sabina, whose prices are read every day.

Follow how a perfume's price moves

On a perfume's page, one line per shop shows how the price has moved over 30 days, 90 days or since tracking began, with the lowest price of each period. Days without a reading stay visible: nothing is made up to fill a gap. You can see whether today's price is a real deal or just the usual price.

Get an alert when the price drops

Set an alert on a perfume and a size:

  • a maximum price: you are told as soon as an offer goes below it;
  • a minimum drop in %;
  • a new low, never read before.

Every morning, after the prices are read, your phone compares the day's prices with your thresholds and shows the notification itself, only once per offer as long as its price does not change.

Your collection and your shops

  • My collection: the perfumes you follow and the ones you own, sorted by price, recent drop, price per ml or how close they are to your threshold.
  • Add a shop: paste the link of a perfume page. The app checks at once that the shop allows this page to be read (robots.txt file) and that it publishes the price; if not, it tells you why.
  • A shop that blocks automatic reading is shown as “source unavailable”: it is never worked around.

Shops are third parties: they do not depend on Atria.Eponym and may change their pages or block access to them.

On Android and on iPhone

The app is available on Android (Google Play) and on iPhone (App Store), in 28 languages.

  • On Android: the latest version, with search in the catalogue of the shops followed and the PRO option.
  • On iPhone: price comparison, history, alerts and the collection. On the App Store, the app is still listed under its former name, “Parfum Pocket – perfume prices”. Shop search and the PRO option are for now available only in the Android version.

PRO: an optional subscription

The free version is enough to compare prices and get alerts. On Android, an optional PRO subscription adds a savings counter, unlimited alerts and history, an adviser that compares sizes by price per ml, a budget alert and unlimited shops added by link (free version: 3 active alerts, 90 days of history, 3 shops added by link).

  • Monthly or yearly subscription, never a one-time purchase: your commitment ends with the period paid for.
  • Cancel at any time in your Google Play subscriptions; access stays open until the end of the period already paid for.
  • The price is shown in the app and on Google Play before any purchase.
  • Prices come from third-party shops that do not depend on Atria.Eponym: the list of shops may change.

The details are in the terms of sale.

Your data stays on your phone

  • No account, no advertising, no audience measurement tools.
  • Your collection, your alert thresholds and your notifications are stored only on your phone.
  • Alerts are worked out by the phone itself: your thresholds are sent to no server.

The details are in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Perfume Price Tracker free?

Yes. The app is free and has no ads, on Android as on iPhone. On Android, an optional PRO subscription, monthly or yearly and cancellable at any time, adds features; price comparison and alerts stay free.

Which shops are compared?

Today MyOrigines, Parfumdo and Sabina, read every day. You can add others by pasting the link of a perfume page, if the shop allows that page to be read automatically and publishes its price. A shop that blocks it is shown as “source unavailable”.

Does the compared price include delivery?

Yes. Offers are ranked by total delivered price: the price plus the cheapest home delivery, for one item. If a shop does not publish its delivery costs, the app says so instead of making up a total.

How does the price drop alert work?

You choose a maximum price, a minimum drop in % or a new low. Every morning, after the prices are read, your phone compares the day's prices with your thresholds and tells you only once per offer as long as its price does not change.

Are the prices shown up to date?

Every price shows its shop, its exact size and the date it was read. Prices are read once a day; a price that is too old is greyed out and never shown as current.

Are promo codes taken into account?

No. The app compares public prices only, the ones every visitor sees in the shop: no promo codes, no members-only prices.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app asks for neither a name nor an email address. Your collection and your alerts stay on your phone; uninstalling the app erases them.

Independent app: it is not affiliated with any shop or perfume brand. Perfume names visible in the screenshots belong to their owners; the prices in the screenshots are real readings from 27 October 2026, not current prices.
