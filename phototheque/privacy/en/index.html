Privacy Policy — Photothèque

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The essentials: Photothèque runs entirely on your computer. It requires no account, uses no server and sends no photo, no file and no data to Atria.Eponym or anyone else.

What the app reads

Photothèque reads only the drives and folders you point it to, in order to compute each file's fingerprint, read the date the photo was taken and compare images. It never modifies, moves or deletes these files.

What it writes

It writes only to the new photo library and to the backup copy you choose, plus its own working index, stored in the app's data folder on your computer. You can delete these files at any time.

What leaves your computer

Nothing, except the following. The subscription goes through your device's store (Microsoft Store on Windows, App Store on Mac): the store processes the payment, under its own privacy rules, and the app only asks it whether a subscription is active. The links on the “About” screen open your browser or your email app, when you ask them to.

What the app does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no error reports sent automatically. Atria.Eponym receives no personal data.

Contact

Atria.Eponym, independent developer based in France — atria.eponym@gmail.com
