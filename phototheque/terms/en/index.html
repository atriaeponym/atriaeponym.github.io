Terms of Sale — Photothèque

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The essentials: reading your drives and viewing the report are free. Everything else requires a subscription with no commitment, cancelable at any time, at no cost. Atria.Eponym's commitment is limited to the paid period.

What the subscription includes

During the paid period: the verified copy to the new photo library, sorting of near-identical photos, the completeness certificate and the backup copy. Adding sources and viewing the report remain free, without a subscription.

Price and duration

  • Monthly: €2.99 per month in France, renewed every month. No commitment.
  • Yearly: €24.99 per year in France, paid for the year, renewed every year. No commitment.

In other countries, the store shows the price in your currency before any purchase.

Canceling

You can cancel at any time, at no cost: on Windows in your Microsoft account (account.microsoft.com/services), on Mac in the App Store subscription settings. Access stays open until the end of the period already paid; there is no automatic refund for the current period.

Refunds

Payments are collected by Microsoft or Apple: refund requests are made to them, under their terms.

Atria.Eponym's commitment

Atria.Eponym keeps the app working during the paid period, with no commitment beyond it. Photothèque does not depend on any third-party service other than the store that manages the subscription: everything happens on your computer, and the photo library you create belongs to you, readable without the app.
