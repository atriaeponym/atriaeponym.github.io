Privacy policy — Squash Call

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: Squash Call can be used without an account. No advertising, no audience measurement, no tracking, no sale of data. What leaves your phone is used only to make the app work, and everything is described below.

Where the data goes

To Supabase, in a data centre in Paris (European Union). Notifications go through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), sign-in e-mails through Resend.

Without an account

  • Notifications: the phone's notification identifier (no name, no e-mail), the language, your notification settings and the matches you follow. It is erased as soon as Firebase reports it as invalid, for example after the app is uninstalled.
  • Full-screen announcements: a number drawn at random at installation, so that an announcement is shown only once.
  • Your favourite players and your “Your call” choices stay on the phone.

With an account (optional)

The account keeps your predictions, your “Your call” choices and your profile. It is created with your e-mail address, without a password: you receive a link. The server keeps:

  • your e-mail address, never visible to others;
  • your profile: username, avatar (a drawing, or a photo from your gallery, reduced on the phone and then uploaded to the server), a colour drawn at random, your choice of animations;
  • your predictions (winner and score), and a points total that the server calculates but displays nowhere;
  • your “Your call” choices (No let, Let or Stroke), with the referee's decision, the game and the score at that moment.

When you are signed in, the phone is linked to your account; it no longer is as soon as you sign out.

What others see

  • Signed-in users can read your profile: username, avatar, colour, animation and notification settings, points total. The app shows only the username and the avatar. Your profile photo is stored at a public web address.
  • Your predictions and your “Your call” choices are shown only to you. Once the match is over, others see only the number of fans for each choice.

Fan animations

When you tap a celebration button, the phone sends live to all connected phones: the type of animation, the match, your username (“Fan” without an account), your drawn avatar and your colour. These animations are not stored.

Crash reports

After a shutdown caused by an error, the app sends the next time it starts: its version, the phone model, the Android version and the technical trace of the error. No account, no e-mail: this report is used only to fix the error.

Sources, images and links

Results, draws, schedules and rankings come from the public squash websites, through the Squash Call server. Player portraits come directly from those websites' servers, city photos from Wikimedia Commons: these sites see your phone's IP address, as with any web page. The links to the rules of squash open your browser.

On your phone

Your account session, a copy of the latest data and images, your favourite players, your “Your call” choices and a prediction made before signing in. This data is excluded from the Android backup and disappears when the app is uninstalled.

Deleting your data

Settings (your avatar, top right) › Account › “Delete my account” immediately and permanently erases your account, your e-mail address, your profile and its photo, your predictions and your “Your call” choices. The matches you follow stay on the phone, with no link to you. Without an account, uninstalling the app is enough.

What the app does not do

No advertising, no audience measurement, no tracking, no sale or commercial sharing of your data. It asks neither for your location, nor your contacts, nor access to your files: a photo goes through the Android picker, which gives it only the chosen image.

Your rights

Access, correction or deletion of your data at any time, by writing to the address below. You may also contact the CNIL (cnil.fr).
